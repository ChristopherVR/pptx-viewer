# Viewing Presentations

This page covers reading a presentation: moving between slides, zooming, hidden slides, and speaker notes. For how to open a file in the first place, see [Opening a file](/user/#opening-a-file).

Once a file is loaded, the active slide renders on the **slide canvas** in the centre, with a strip of thumbnails in the **slides panel** on the left.

![Slide canvas showing a loaded presentation](/user-guide/viewing-slide-canvas.jpg)

Charts, tables, shapes, SmartArt, and media all render as live content, so you can select text on the slide and copy it like any web page.

![A chart rendered on a slide](/docs-shots/chart-slide.jpg)

## Navigating slides

Move between slides in any of these ways:

- **Slides panel** - click any thumbnail on the left to jump straight to that slide.
- **Arrow keys** - with nothing selected on the canvas, press **Left Arrow** / **Right Arrow** to step to the previous / next slide.
- **Status bar** - the counter at the bottom left reads **Slide x of y** so you always know where you are.

![Navigating between slides](/user-guide/viewing-navigation.gif)

The slides panel highlights the current slide with a coloured bar on its left edge.

::: tip Arrow keys do double duty
In edit mode the arrow keys nudge the selected element. They only change slides when nothing is selected. Press **Escape** first to deselect.
:::

## Zooming and fitting

The zoom controls sit at the right end of the **status bar**:

| Control                     | What it does                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Zoom out** (minus button) | Decreases the zoom in steps.                                                         |
| **Percentage button**       | Shows the current zoom level; click it to **zoom to fit** the available canvas area. |
| **Zoom in** (plus button)   | Increases the zoom in steps.                                                         |

![Zoom controls in the status bar](/user-guide/viewing-status-bar.jpg)

You can also:

- Hold **Ctrl** (Cmd on macOS) and scroll the mouse wheel over the canvas to zoom smoothly around the pointer.
- Use **View tab > Zoom to Fit** in the ribbon.
- Pinch to zoom on touch devices.

## View modes

The status bar has three view buttons:

- **Normal view** - the standard layout with the slides panel, canvas, and inspector.
- **Slide sorter** - a full-screen grid of every slide.
- **Slide show** - starts a fullscreen presentation (see [Presenting](/user/presenting)).

### The slide sorter

The slide sorter is the best way to get an overview of a large deck:

1. Click **Slide sorter** in the status bar.
2. Browse the grid. When the deck has sections, slides are grouped under their section headings.
3. **Double-click** a slide to open it in normal view, or press **Escape** to close the sorter.

Inside the sorter you can also:

- Drag slides to reorder them (edit mode).
- Click to select, **Shift-click** to select a range, and right-click for slide actions (duplicate, hide, delete; see [Managing slides](/user/editing#managing-slides)).
- Zoom the thumbnails with the slider in the bottom toolbar, or press **Ctrl +** / **Ctrl -**.

## Hidden slides

Slides marked as hidden stay in the file but are skipped during a slideshow.

- In the slides panel and sorter they appear dimmed with a diagonal hatch pattern and an eye-off icon.
- You can still open, read, and edit a hidden slide normally.
- To hide or unhide slides, right-click them in the slide sorter and choose **Hide Slides** or **Show Slides** (edit mode).

## Reading speaker notes

1. Click the **Notes** button in the status bar to open the notes panel below the canvas.
2. The current slide's speaker notes appear there; scroll if they are long.
3. Click **Notes** again to close the panel.

During a slideshow, notes are shown to you (not the audience) in **presenter view**; see [Presenting](/user/presenting#presenter-view).
